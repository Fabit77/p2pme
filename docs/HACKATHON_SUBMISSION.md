# Hackathon submission

- Name: Fondo
- Tagline: Cobros simples para comunidades.
- Problem: small organizations mix community money with personal accounts and manually reconcile hundreds of screenshots.
- Solution: create a campaign, share one link, receive local payments and automatically associate every payment with a participant and purpose.
- P2P.me: official SDK/widgets route ARS local payments through a registered integrator to USDC settlement on Base Sepolia.
